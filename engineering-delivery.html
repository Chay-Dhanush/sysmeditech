How site delivery is controlled

From coordinated drawings to an inspectable installation

Engineering delivery is the site process that turns the approved design into an installation that can be inspected, tested, recorded and maintained.

01

Drawing & interface control

Coordinate shop drawings, builders' work openings, ceiling/shaft routes and equipment access before work is closed in.

02

Sequencing & site execution

Plan work fronts, material arrival, specialist-vendor interfaces and inspection hold points to reduce clashes and rework.

03

Quality & completion tracking

Track installation observations, non-conformances, corrective actions, test readiness and the close-out items agreed for handover.

Inspection & commissioning

Evidence to collect during site execution

A single project test register should identify the system, location, method, acceptance criteria, instrument and responsible signatory for each applicable check.

01 · BEFORE CONCEALMENT

Inspection hold points

Record supports, material identification, route segregation, access and inspection approvals before ceilings, shafts or trenches are closed.

02 · SYSTEM TESTS

Specialist test records

Collect relevant pressure, leak, electrical, airflow, drainage and equipment test reports from the responsible specialist contractor.

03 · FUNCTION

Integrated functional checks

Coordinate start-up and interface checks for controls, alarms, source changeover and essential services where they are part of the scope.

04 · DEFECTS

Snag & corrective-action closure

Log the issue, location, responsible party, action taken and re-inspection result; close items only when evidence is recorded.

05 · TRACEABILITY

Materials & instruments

Retain approved material submittals, equipment data and calibration details for test instruments used in recorded results.

These are typical verification points, not a claim that every test has been completed on every project. The approved design, applicable code, equipment manufacturer and contract scope determine the required tests, acceptance limits and authorised sign-off.

Project records

A structured handover, not just a finished site

The close-out package should be indexed by discipline and location so the facility team can operate and maintain the installation.

Project close-out

  • Approved and as-built drawings with revision status
  • Inspection, material approval and snag-closure registers
  • Equipment schedules, warranties and O&M manuals

Commissioning close-out

  • System test reports and calibration references
  • Start-up / functional check and training records
  • Outstanding-items list with agreed owner and status
