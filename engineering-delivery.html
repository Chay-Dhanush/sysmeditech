What it is

What this service covers

The service is planned around the facility, its intended use, technical requirements and agreed project scope.

  • Site-level coordination of MEP, electrical, plumbing and allied installation works.
  • Work sequencing and interface management across service routes, ceilings, shafts and equipment areas.
  • Progress communication, quality checks and close-out planning based on the contract scope.
Why it matters

Why it is important in healthcare facilities

Healthcare spaces depend on several building systems working together. A coordinated approach helps those systems support clinical work reliably.

  • Healthcare projects often involve many services sharing limited space and working around operational constraints.
  • Coordinated execution helps identify clashes, dependencies and schedule risks earlier.
  • Clear checks and handover planning help the facility team understand what has been completed and what remains in scope.
