At each bed space

Utilities arranged around patient care

An ICU or NICU bed space is a working service zone. The locations and quantities of outlets, power, communication and equipment interfaces should follow the approved bed layout.

01

Bedhead units & service pendants

Medical-gas terminals, electrical sockets, data and nurse-call points arranged for the planned bed position and clinical workflow.

02

Equipment utility interfaces

Utility locations and clearances coordinated around monitors, ventilators, infusion equipment and other devices identified by the clinical brief.

03

NICU cot / incubator bays

Incubator and warmer positions, service reach, power access and circulation space planned so staff can reach equipment and the infant without obstructing care.

Inspection & commissioning

ICU and NICU bed-space verification

Check the complete bed position with the actual room schedule and the responsible clinical and specialist teams.

01 · POINT CHECK

Headwall / pendant point-to-point

Match every outlet, socket, data point and call point to the approved bed-space schedule; verify labels, position and physical access.

02 · MEDICAL GAS

Gas outlets & alarms

Use the medical-gas page's specified identity, pressure/flow and alarm checks; retain the authorised test records for each designated point.

03 · ELECTRICAL

Socket, earth & backup supply

Where included in scope, verify polarity, protective earth/continuity and changeover of essential or UPS-backed circuits.

04 · CONNECTIVITY

Nurse call & data

Test call initiation, indication and reset, plus the data/network link that is part of the installed scope.

05 · ROOM CONDITION

Ventilation & environment

Record temperature, airflow and pressure criteria where these are specified for the unit, especially where NICU room zoning is defined.

These are typical verification points, not a claim that every test has been completed on every project. The approved design, applicable code, equipment manufacturer and contract scope determine the required tests, acceptance limits and authorised sign-off.

Verification pack
