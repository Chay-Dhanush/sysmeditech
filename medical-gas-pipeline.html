Medical Gas Pipeline Systems

Medical gas infrastructure is an essential utility network in hospitals, supporting the connection between central supply equipment and clinical outlets.

SYS Medi Tech coordinates medical gas pipeline scope and associated equipment interfaces around the approved design, facility requirements and applicable project specifications.

“From the source plant to each clinical outlet, every gas line must be identified, traceable and verified.”

System breakdown

From the gas source to the clinical outlet

A medical gas pipeline is a connected distribution system. Each part has a distinct role, and the installation must follow the approved gas schedule and drawings.

01

Source equipment & changeover

Manifold or supply connection, pressure regulation and primary/secondary source arrangement where included in the design.

02

Copper distribution & zone isolation

Gas-specific copper pipework, supports, identification, isolation valves and zone valve service units routed to the designated clinical areas.

03

Terminal units & clinical points

Gas-specific terminal units at bedheads, pendants, operating theatres and other locations shown on the approved outlet schedule.
