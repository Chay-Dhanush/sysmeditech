Critical Care Infrastructure

Engineering services for operating theatres, ICU, NICU and other high-dependency areas

Critical-care environments need carefully coordinated infrastructure to support clinical equipment, environmental control, essential services and staff workflows. The exact design and installation requirements depend on the facility, clinical brief and applicable project standards.

What it is

What this service covers

The service is planned around the facility, its intended use, technical requirements and agreed project scope.

  • HVAC and cooling coordination for operating theatres and critical-care rooms.
  • Medical gas pipeline and related utility coordination according to the agreed design.
  • Integration of electrical, lighting, plumbing and other building services.
Why it matters

Why it is important in healthcare facilities

Healthcare spaces depend on several building systems working together. A coordinated approach helps those systems support clinical work reliably.

  • Critical-care rooms support time-sensitive clinical work and require dependable building services.
  • Ventilation, environmental conditions and utility interfaces must be planned around the facility’s design and operational needs.
  • Coordination and verification help teams identify installation issues before the space is handed over.
