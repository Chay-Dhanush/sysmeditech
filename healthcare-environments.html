Healthcare Environments

Integrated infrastructure for hospitals and healthcare facilities

What it is

What this service covers

The service is planned around the facility, its intended use, technical requirements and agreed project scope.

  • Coordinated medical gas pipeline, HVAC, MEP, electrical and plumbing scopes based on project needs.
  • Engineering planning for operating theatres, wards, diagnostic spaces and support areas.
  • Coordination of service routes, access, installation sequence and maintainability.
Why it matters

Why it is important in healthcare facilities

Healthcare spaces depend on several building systems working together. A coordinated approach helps those systems support clinical work reliably.

  • Clinical facilities rely on building systems that support day-to-day care and safe workflows.
  • Early coordination can reduce clashes between trades and help avoid avoidable rework.
  • Well-planned infrastructure supports maintenance access and future facility operations.
