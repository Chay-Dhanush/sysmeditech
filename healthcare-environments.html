Plan the room around its use

Clinical, diagnostic and support spaces are not interchangeable

Each space has a different clinical workflow and service requirement. The room brief should set the utility points, equipment interfaces, circulation and maintenance access before routes are fixed.

01

Treatment & procedure rooms

Coordinate medical-gas points, ventilation, electrical supply and equipment positions to the room's approved clinical function.

02

Wards & patient areas

Plan bedhead outlets, lighting, power, nurse-call/data interfaces and accessible service routes around beds and staff movement.

03

Diagnostics & support areas

Allow for the equipment load, dedicated utilities, ventilation, water/drainage and service access specified for the equipment and room.

Inspection & commissioning

A room-readiness checklist by service

The final room walk-through should follow the room data sheet and check the utilities actually installed in that space.

01 · MEDICAL GAS

Outlets & zone isolation

Check the room's specified gas terminals, labels and zone isolation arrangement, with test evidence from the authorised medical-gas process.

02 · HVAC

Air delivery & room conditions

Where mechanical ventilation is specified, verify terminal airflow, room temperature and any required pressure relationship.

03 · ELECTRICAL

Power & lighting

Confirm socket locations, circuit identification, lighting operation and required protective/essential-supply tests.

04 · PLUMBING

Water & drainage

Inspect fixtures, water connections, drainage falls, traps, isolation and leak-free operation where included.

05 · COORDINATION

Access & maintainability

Check equipment clearance, access panels, isolation points and that one service does not block another.

These are typical verification points, not a claim that every test has been completed on every project. The approved design, applicable code, equipment manufacturer and contract scope determine the required tests, acceptance limits and authorised sign-off.

Completion evidence
