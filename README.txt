SYS MEDI TECH WEBSITE PACKAGE

Preview locally by opening index.html. For Cloudflare Pages, deploy the contents of this folder so index.html is at the project root alongside the three detail pages and assets/ directory.

The three focus cards open detailed pages explaining what each area covers, why it matters in healthcare facilities, and how SYS Medi Tech can deliver the work. The logo is available as a scalable SVG and a high-resolution transparent PNG.

Before going live: replace the sample testimonial text with approved customer feedback; set the companyEmail constant in the contact-form JavaScript to your official company email; review company claims and service scope. The enquiry form validates name, email and one-or-more service selections, then opens a pre-filled email draft. For direct online form submission without an email app, connect a form backend/service.

Suggested positioning phrase: Assess. Engineer. Deliver. It is more specific to technical healthcare infrastructure work than a generic “3D approach”.
